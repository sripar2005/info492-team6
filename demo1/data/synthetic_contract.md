# Agreement Between Owner and Construction Manager as Constructor (GC/CM)

*Modeled on AIA Document A133-2009, with Owner modifications. Guaranteed Maximum Price.*

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** This is a fictional contract written for INFO 492 C coursework. It is modeled on a publicly documented school GC/CM project, but it is not that project's actual contract.

**Project:** School Modernization and Addition (the "Project")
**Owner:** Example School District No. 1 ("Owner")
**Construction Manager:** Example Builders, Inc. ("CM")
**Architect:** Example Architecture, PLLC ("Architect")
**Effective Date:** June 17, 2019
**Guaranteed Maximum Price:** $26,240,507

## Article 1. General Provisions

**1.1 Contract Documents.** The Contract Documents are this Agreement, Exhibits A through E, the Drawings and Specifications (including Division 01, excerpted in Exhibit E), Addenda, and Modifications issued after the Effective Date.

**1.2 Order of Precedence.** If the Contract Documents conflict, they control in this order: (a) executed Modifications; (b) Articles 1 through 14; (c) Exhibits A through D; (d) Exhibit E; (e) Drawings and other Specifications. Exhibit E may add procedural detail, but it does not shorten any notice period or lengthen any response time stated in the Articles.

**1.3 Definitions.**

- *Change Order (CO)*: a written instrument signed by Owner, Architect, and CM that adjusts the GMP, the Contract Time, or both.
- *Change Proposal (CP)*: CM's priced request for an adjustment to the GMP or Contract Time.
- *Construction Change Directive (CCD)*: a written order signed by Owner directing a change before the adjustment is agreed.
- *Architect's Supplemental Instruction (ASI)*: a written instruction by Architect that is not intended to change cost or time.
- *RFI*: a written request from CM to Architect for clarification of the Contract Documents.
- *Potential Change*: any RFI response, ASI, direction, condition, or event that CM believes will increase cost or time.
- *Specified General Conditions (SGC)*: the fixed-price field management and site costs listed in Exhibit C.
- *Negotiated Support Services (NSS)*: the support services listed in Exhibit C and priced as lump sums.
- *Self-Performed Work (SPW)*: Work performed by CM's own forces.

**1.4 Time Periods.** "Days" means calendar days unless stated otherwise. If a period ends on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday, it ends on the next business day.

## Article 2. CM's Responsibilities

**2.1 Services.** CM shall provide construction management and general contracting services for the Project as a GC/CM and shall furnish all labor, materials, and equipment needed to complete the Work.

**2.2 Review of Documents.** CM shall carefully study the Contract Documents and shall promptly report to Architect, as an RFI, any error, inconsistency, or omission it discovers. CM is not responsible for errors in the Contract Documents unless it knowingly failed to report them.

**2.3 No Authority to Change Scope.** CM shall not perform changed Work, and no RFI response, ASI, meeting minute, or oral direction authorizes added cost or time, until a CO or CCD is issued. Work on a change without a CO or CCD is at CM's risk.

## Article 3. Contract Time

**3.1 Commencement.** CM shall begin the Work on the date stated in the Notice to Proceed, issued by Owner.

**3.2 Substantial Completion.** CM shall achieve Substantial Completion within 540 days after the Notice to Proceed, and Final Completion within 60 days after Substantial Completion.

**3.3 Liquidated Damages.** If CM fails to achieve Substantial Completion on time, CM shall pay Owner $2,500 for each day of delay.

**3.4 Time Extensions.** Contract Time is extended only by CO. An extension is available only for delays outside CM's control that are shown on the critical path, and only if CM has given notice under Section 6.2.

## Article 4. Guaranteed Maximum Price

**4.1 GMP.** The Guaranteed Maximum Price is $26,240,507. It covers the Cost of the Work, CM's Fee, SGC, NSS, and CM Contingency, itemized in Exhibit A. CM shall pay any cost that would cause the GMP to be exceeded, unless a CO has adjusted the GMP.

**4.2 Savings.** Any savings in the GMP belong to Owner.

**4.3 CM Contingency.** The CM Contingency is $338,007. CM may use it only for costs within CM's reasonable control, such as subcontract buyout variances and CM's own coordination errors, and only with Owner's written approval before the cost is incurred. The CM Contingency may not be used for Owner-requested changes or for RFI or ASI changes, which are handled under Article 6.

**4.4 Adjustments.** The GMP is adjusted only by CO.

## Article 5. Compensation and Costs

**5.1 CM's Fee.** CM's Fee is 5% of the sum of the Subcontract Work and SPW shown in Exhibit A, which is $1,122,500. The Fee is fixed and changes only as stated in Article 6.

**5.2 Specified General Conditions.** SGC is $1,720,000, billed as shown in Exhibit C. SGC may be increased only by CO for an extension of Contract Time, at a daily rate of $3,185 per day. No Fee is added to SGC.

**5.3 Negotiated Support Services.** NSS is $610,000, billed as shown in Exhibit C. No Fee is added to NSS, and NSS changes only by CO.

**5.4 Reimbursable Costs.** Reimbursable Cost of the Work includes subcontract payments, materials, CM labor at the rates in the approved labor schedule plus a fixed labor burden of 32% of direct wages, bond and insurance premiums at actual cost, Washington B&O taxes at actual cost, and permit fees.

**5.5 Costs Covered by the Fee or SGC.** The following are covered by the Fee or SGC and shall not be billed separately, including on a CP: home office overhead; estimating, accounting, and purchasing; salaries of CM's executives; the supervisory and management positions listed in Exhibit C; field office and project vehicles; hand tools and small tools under $1,000 per item; and the cost of preparing, pricing, and negotiating Change Proposals.

**5.6 Self-Performed Work.** SPW shall not exceed 30% of the Subcontract Work and SPW combined (excluding SGC and NSS) over the life of the Project. CM may self-perform only trades for which its sealed bid was the lowest responsive bid. CM shall report the running SPW percentage with each Application for Payment.

**5.7 Subcontracting.** CM shall competitively bid all Subcontract Work. CM shall give Owner at least 10 days' written notice before awarding any subcontract to a firm related to CM through ownership, management, or family ties.

## Article 6. Changes in the Work

**6.1 Types of Change.** Changes are made by Change Order, Construction Change Directive, or Architect's Supplemental Instruction.

**6.2 Notice of Potential Change.** CM shall give Owner and Architect written notice of a Potential Change within the following periods:

- (a) 7 days after CM receives an RFI response, ASI, or submittal return that CM believes changes cost or time;
- (b) 7 days after CM receives a written or oral direction from Owner or Architect that CM believes is a change;
- (c) before disturbing the condition, and no later than 7 days after first observing it, for concealed or unknown physical conditions that differ from the Contract Documents;
- (d) 14 days after CM first knew or should have known of any other event that may increase cost or time.

The notice shall describe the event, identify the document or direction involved, and give a preliminary estimate of cost and time. If CM does not give notice within the applicable period, CM waives any adjustment for costs incurred before notice is given and may waive any adjustment of time.

**6.3 Change Proposals.** After a notice under Section 6.2, CM shall submit a CP within 21 days. If Architect issues a Proposal Request, CM shall submit a CP within 14 days after receiving it (see Exhibit E). Each CP shall:

- reference the RFI, ASI, direction, or CCD that caused it, and the notice date;
- give a breakdown of labor hours and rates, materials, equipment, subcontractor pricing, markup, and Fee;
- include subcontractor quotes and signed T&M tags for any time-and-material work;
- state any claimed time impact in days.

**6.4 Review of Change Proposals.** Owner, through Architect, shall respond to a complete CP within 14 days after receipt. An incomplete CP shall be returned within 7 days with the missing items identified. Silence is not acceptance. If Owner has not responded after 14 days, CM may send a written reminder and treat the lack of response as a basis for a claim under Article 10.

**6.5 Pricing Rules.**

- **6.5.1 Direct costs.** Direct costs are labor with the fixed burden in Section 5.4, materials at actual cost net of discounts, equipment under Section 6.5.4, and subcontract costs.
- **6.5.2 Subcontractor markup.** A subcontractor may add overhead and profit of up to 10% on its own direct cost and up to 5% on the cost of work performed by its lower-tier subcontractors. Total markup on any portion of the work shall not exceed 15%.
- **6.5.3 CM's Fee on changes.** CM may add a Fee of 5% to the direct costs and subcontractor markups of a change. No Fee is added to NSS, SGC adjustments, bond and insurance premiums, B&O taxes, or permit fees. On deductive changes, the Fee is reduced at the same 5% rate.
- **6.5.4 Equipment.** Equipment listed as included in SGC in Exhibit B shall not be charged to a CP. Other CM-owned equipment is charged at 75% of the current published Blue Book rental rate, and rented equipment at the invoiced cost with no markup other than the Fee. Equipment is charged only for the hours it is actually used on the changed Work, supported by daily logs. Idle equipment is charged at 50% of the applicable rate and only when the idle time is caused by the change.
- **6.5.5 Costs not chargeable.** The items in Section 5.5 shall not be included in a CP.
- **6.5.6 Time impacts.** A time extension is compensated only for delays caused by Owner or Architect that are on the critical path, using the SGC daily rate in Section 5.2 and with no Fee. Delays with concurrent causes receive time but no compensation.
- **6.5.7 Time-and-material work.** T&M tags shall be submitted within 3 business days after the work is performed and signed by CM's superintendent and Owner's Representative. Unsigned tags are not accepted as backup.

**6.6 Construction Change Directives.** Owner may issue a CCD directing CM to proceed with a change before the price is agreed. CM shall proceed. CM shall submit its CP within 14 days after receiving the CCD. If the parties have not agreed within 30 days after the CP is submitted, the cost is determined as the actual Cost of the Work calculated under Section 6.5, plus the Fee, and CM may pursue any disagreement as a claim. Within 45 days after the price is agreed, the CCD shall be converted to a CO.

**6.7 Approval of Change Orders.** A CO is effective only when signed by Owner, Architect, and CM. Owner's Representative may approve a CO of up to $250,000. A CO above $250,000, or a series of related changes totaling more than $250,000, requires approval by Owner's Board of Directors, and CM shall allow time for the Board's schedule when planning the change.

**6.8 Supplemental Instructions.** Architect may issue ASIs that do not change cost or time. If CM believes an ASI will change cost or time, it shall give notice under Section 6.2(a) before proceeding. Proceeding with an ASI does not waive CM's right to notice, if notice is timely.

**6.9 Change Log.** CM shall keep a log of all CPs, numbered in sequence, showing for each the date of notice, date submitted, amount, status, and the originating RFI, ASI, or CCD. CM shall update the log weekly and submit it with each Application for Payment.

## Article 7. RFIs and Communications

**7.1 Submitting RFIs.** CM shall submit RFIs on the form in Exhibit E. Each RFI shall identify the drawing sheets and specification sections involved, state the question, propose CM's recommended answer, and give the date by which a response is needed.

**7.2 Review Before Submission.** CM shall review the Contract Documents before submitting an RFI. If Architect determines that an RFI was answered in the Contract Documents, Architect may return it without a full response. Architect's review costs for each such RFI beyond the tenth shall be charged to CM at $350 per RFI by deduction from the next Application for Payment.

**7.3 Response Time.** Architect shall respond to each RFI within 10 days after receipt. An RFI marked Critical Path shall be answered within 3 business days. If Architect cannot respond in time, it shall notify CM within 5 days and give the date a response will be provided.

**7.4 Effect of Responses.** An RFI response does not change the Contract Sum or Contract Time. If CM believes a response changes cost or time, CM shall follow Sections 6.2 and 6.3.

**7.5 Distribution.** CM shall send each RFI response to every affected subcontractor within 2 business days. CM shall keep an RFI log, updated weekly, showing the date each RFI was sent and answered.

**7.6 Unanswered RFIs.** If an RFI is unanswered after 10 days, CM may send a written follow-up. If it is unanswered after 15 days, CM may give notice of a Potential Change under Section 6.2(d).

**7.7 Meeting Minutes.** Minutes of coordination meetings are for record only. A statement in meeting minutes is not notice under Section 6.2 and does not authorize a change unless it is repeated in a written notice, CO, or CCD.

## Article 8. Submittals

**8.1 Submittal Schedule.** CM shall submit a submittal schedule within 30 days after the Notice to Proceed, identifying long-lead items.

**8.2 Review Times.** Architect shall review each submittal within 14 days after receipt and each resubmittal within 10 days. CM shall review and stamp each submittal for compliance and coordination before sending it to Architect.

**8.3 Approved as Noted.** When a submittal is returned as "Approved as Noted" or "Revise and Resubmit," CM shall tell every affected subcontractor within 3 business days. If CM believes a comment changes cost or time, CM shall give notice under Section 6.2(a).

## Article 9. Payments

**9.1 Applications.** CM shall submit an Application for Payment by the 25th of each month for Work completed through that date, with the change log, the SPW percentage, and lien waivers.

**9.2 Payment.** Owner shall pay the approved amount within 30 days after receiving a complete Application. Overdue undisputed amounts bear interest at 1% per month.

**9.3 Retainage.** Owner shall retain 5% of each progress payment as required by Washington law.

**9.4 Changes.** CM may bill a change only after a CO is executed or a CCD is issued. CCD work may be billed as costs are incurred, subject to adjustment when the price is agreed.

**9.5 Final Payment.** Final payment is due after Final Completion, receipt of all closeout documents, and the lapse of statutory lien periods.

## Article 10. Claims and Disputes

**10.1 Claim Notice.** A claim for added cost or time shall be made in writing within 21 days after the event giving rise to it, or within 14 days after CM receives Owner's response to a CP that CM disputes, whichever applies. A claim not made in time is waived.

**10.2 Decision.** Owner shall respond to a claim within 30 days after receiving it.

**10.3 Mediation and Litigation.** Unresolved claims shall go to mediation, and then to the Superior Court of King County, Washington. CM shall continue the Work during any dispute.

## Article 11. Records and Audit

**11.1 Records.** CM shall keep all cost records for at least 6 years after final payment.

**11.2 Audit.** Owner or its auditor may audit CM's records at any time during that period. Any charge found not allowable under Articles 5 and 6 shall be credited to Owner within 30 days after the audit report, and Owner may offset the amount against money owed to CM.

## Article 12. Insurance and Bonds

**12.1** CM shall furnish payment and performance bonds each for 100% of the GMP, and commercial general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Owner as additional insured.

## Article 13. Termination

**13.1 For Cause.** Owner may terminate for cause if CM fails to cure a material default within 7 days after written notice.

**13.2 For Convenience.** Owner may terminate for convenience on 14 days' written notice and shall pay CM for Work performed and reasonable demobilization costs.

## Article 14. Miscellaneous

**14.1 Governing Law.** Washington law governs this Agreement.

**14.2 Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and sent to the representatives in Exhibit D. A notice sent through the project management platform designated by Owner is a written notice and is received on the next business day.

**14.3 No Waiver.** Owner's payment or failure to object does not waive any right under this Agreement.

## Exhibit E. Division 01 Excerpts

**Section 01 26 00. Contract Modification Procedures.**

1. *Proposal Requests.* Architect may issue a written Proposal Request describing a possible change. CM shall return a CP within 14 days after receiving it, or within that time explain why more time is needed.
2. *CP numbering.* CPs are numbered in sequence (CP-0001, CP-0002, and so on). A revised CP keeps its number with a letter suffix.
3. *Backup.* Every CP shall attach the RFI, ASI, or CCD that caused it, subcontractor quotes with their own breakdowns, equipment logs, and T&M tags.
4. *Pay application link.* The CP log is submitted with each Application for Payment, as required by Section 6.9.

**Section 01 31 00. Project Management and Coordination.**

1. *RFI form.* Each RFI has a unique number, date, sheet and spec references, the question, CM's proposed answer, and the needed-by date.
2. *Critical Path RFIs.* RFIs marked Critical Path shall state the activity affected and the date the activity will be delayed if no answer is received.
3. *Coordination meetings.* CM shall hold weekly coordination meetings with the Architect and Owner's Representative and issue minutes within 2 business days. Minutes are not notices, consistent with Section 7.7.

**Section 01 33 00. Submittal Procedures.**

1. *Submittal numbering.* Each submittal uses its specification section number and a sequence number.
2. *Long-lead items.* Items with a lead time over 12 weeks shall be flagged on the submittal schedule and submitted within 45 days after the Notice to Proceed.
3. *Stamps.* CM shall stamp each submittal before sending it to Architect, confirming it has been checked for compliance and coordination.
