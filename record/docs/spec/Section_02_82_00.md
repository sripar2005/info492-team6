## 1.01.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Summary. Removal and disposal of asbestos-containing materials (ACM) identified in Owner's hazardous materials survey, in the existing building areas shown on the abatement drawings.

## 1.03.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Hazardous materials survey. Owner's survey identifies ACM in Wing A floor tile, pipe insulation in Wing B, and gym roofing mastic. The survey did not identify mastic under flooring in classrooms 110 to 116. Quantities in the survey are for information; CM shall verify before abatement.

## 1.04.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Suspect materials not in the survey. If CM or the abatement contractor finds suspect material not identified in the survey, stop work in the area, isolate it, sample it, and notify Owner and Architect the same day. Notice of a differing condition is governed by Section 6.2(c) of the Agreement.

## 3.04.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Waste handling and disposal. The abatement contractor shall package ACM waste in labeled, sealed containers, transport it in an enclosed vehicle, and dispose of it at a licensed landfill. Waste manifests shall be submitted to Owner within 10 days of disposal.

## 3.06.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Restoration. Patching and painting of surfaces disturbed by abatement are not part of this Section; restoration is by the trade sections only where shown on the Drawings.
