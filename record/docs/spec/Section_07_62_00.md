## 1.01.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Summary. Parapet copings, counterflashings, through-wall flashings, and storefront and window head flashings.

## 2.01.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Material. Prefinished galvanized steel sheet, ASTM A653, G90 coating, with a two-coat fluoropolymer finish in the color selected by Architect.

## 2.02.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Copings. Form parapet copings from 24-gauge sheet with continuous cleats and concealed splice plates at joints.

## 2.02.B
*2019-05-17, architect*

Counterflashings. Form counterflashings from 24-gauge sheet, set in reglets or surface-mounted with termination bar and sealant.

## 3.01.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Installation. Install flashing to SMACNA Architectural Sheet Metal Manual details, with expansion joints at 10 feet on center maximum and lapped, sealed joints.

## 3.02.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Head flashings. Provide head flashings with end dams and drip edges at every storefront and window opening, turned up behind the weather barrier at least 4 inches.
