# Section 22 14 26 Roof Drains

## 1.01.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Summary. Primary roof drains, overflow roof drains, and their leaders to the building storm drain or to daylight.

## 2.01.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Primary roof drains. Cast iron body with flashing clamp, underdeck clamp, and cast iron dome, sized as shown on the plumbing drawings.

## 2.02.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Overflow roof drains. Provide where indicated on the Drawings, with a 2-inch water dam above the roof surface. Where none are indicated, none are required.

## 2.03.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Leaders. Service-weight cast iron no-hub pipe and fittings, 4-inch minimum, insulated where above occupied spaces.

## 3.01.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Installation. Set drains level at the low points of the roof, coordinated with the roofing installer, and test leaders to 10 feet of head for 15 minutes.

## 3.02.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Cleanouts. Provide a cleanout at the base of each vertical leader and at each change of direction greater than 45 degrees.
