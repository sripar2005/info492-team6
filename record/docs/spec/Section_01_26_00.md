## 1.01.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Summary. This Section states the procedures for Proposal Requests, Change Proposals (CPs), Construction Change Directives, and Change Orders. Article 6 of the Agreement controls if this Section conflicts with it.

## 1.02.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Proposal Requests. Architect may issue a written Proposal Request describing a possible change. CM shall return a CP within 14 days after receiving it, or within that time explain in writing why more time is needed.

## 1.03.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Change Proposal content. Each CP shall reference the RFI, ASI, direction, or CCD that caused it and the date of CM's notice, and shall include a breakdown of labor hours and rates, materials, equipment, subcontractor pricing, markup, and Fee, and any claimed time impact in days.

## 1.03.B
*2019-05-17, architect*

CP numbering. CPs are numbered in sequence (CP-0001, CP-0002, and so on). A revised CP keeps its number with a letter suffix (CP-0201A).

## 1.04.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Backup. Every CP shall attach the RFI, ASI, or CCD that caused it, subcontractor quotes with their own line-item breakdowns, equipment logs, and T&M tags. A lump-sum subcontractor quote without a breakdown is not acceptable backup.

## 1.05.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

Time-and-material tags. T&M tags shall list workers, hours, equipment, and materials for each day, and shall be signed by CM's superintendent and Owner's Representative and submitted within 3 business days after the work is performed. Tags signed only by a subcontractor's foreman are not accepted.

## 1.06.A
*2019-05-17, architect*

CP log. CM shall keep the CP log required by Section 6.9 of the Agreement and submit it with each Application for Payment.
