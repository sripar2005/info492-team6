# Short Form Subcontract (Earthwork)

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** A fictional subcontract written with AI assistance for INFO 492 C coursework. No real firm, person or project is represented.

**Contractor:** Example Builders, Inc. ("Contractor"), GC/CM for the Project
**Subcontractor:** Granite Earthworks ("Subcontractor")
**Project:** School Modernization and Addition, for Example School District No. 1 ("Owner")
**Prime Contract:** Agreement Between Owner and Construction Manager as Constructor (GC/CM) dated June 17, 2019 (the "Prime Contract")
**Architect:** Example Architecture, PLLC
**Subcontract Sum:** $920,000
**Date:** December 9, 2019

## Article 1. Agreement

Section 1.1 Contractor engages Subcontractor, and Subcontractor agrees, to perform the Subcontract Work for the Subcontract Sum, subject to additions and deductions by written change order.

## Article 2. Scope

Section 2.1 The Subcontract Work is excavation, shoring, over-excavation and structural fill, grading and erosion control for the addition and site, complete and ready for use, including layout, protection of adjacent work, daily cleanup and removal of its own debris.

Section 2.2 Subcontractor has examined the site and the Drawings and Specifications and accepts the existing conditions shown on them.

## Article 3. Contract Documents

Section 3.1 This Subcontract consists of this form, Exhibit 1 (list of Drawings and Specifications), Exhibit 2 (schedule of values) and the Prime Contract to the extent stated in Section 3.2.

Section 3.2 The Prime Contract is listed for reference only, and its notice, claim and change periods do not apply to Subcontractor.

## Article 4. Schedule

Section 4.1 Subcontractor shall start within 5 working days after Contractor's notice to mobilize and shall keep pace with the project schedule.

## Article 5. Payment

Section 5.1 Applications are due on the 20th of each month on Contractor's form, with a schedule of values update and lien waivers.

Section 5.2 Contractor's receipt of payment from Owner is a condition precedent to any payment to Subcontractor for that Work.

Section 5.3 Retainage is 5% of each progress payment.

## Article 6. Changes

Section 6.1 Changes are made only by written change order or written directive signed by Contractor's Project Manager.

Section 6.2 When Contractor requests a price for a change, Subcontractor shall deliver it within 10 days. Late pricing may be determined by Contractor from its own estimate.

Section 6.3 Overhead and profit on changes is limited to 10% of Subcontractor's own direct cost and 5% of lower-tier subcontract cost. Equipment owned by Subcontractor is charged at no more than 75% of the published rental rate.

## Article 7. Claims and Notice

Section 7.1 Notice of any claim for added cost or time shall be given to Contractor in writing no later than 14 days after the event giving rise to it. A notice must state the cause and give a preliminary estimate.

Section 7.2 Claims not noticed within that period are waived.

## Article 8. Delays

Section 8.1 Subcontractor's sole remedy for any delay, whatever its cause, is an extension of the Subcontract Time.

Section 8.2 Subcontractor shall provide added crews at its own cost if it falls behind for reasons within its control.

## Article 9. Insurance

Section 9.1 Commercial general liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate, automobile liability of $1,000,000, and Washington industrial insurance as required by law.

## Article 10. General

Section 10.1 Washington law governs this Subcontract. Venue is King County.

Section 10.2 Subcontractor may not assign this Subcontract or sublet the whole of the Subcontract Work without Contractor's written consent.

Signed for Contractor (Example Builders, Inc.) and for Subcontractor (Granite Earthworks).
