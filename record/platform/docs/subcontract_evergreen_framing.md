# Subcontract Agreement: Framing and drywall

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** A fictional subcontract written with AI assistance for INFO 492 C coursework. No real firm, person or project is represented.

**Contractor:** Example Builders, Inc. ("Contractor"), GC/CM for the Project
**Subcontractor:** Evergreen Framing ("Subcontractor")
**Project:** School Modernization and Addition, for Example School District No. 1 ("Owner")
**Prime Contract:** Agreement Between Owner and Construction Manager as Constructor (GC/CM) dated June 17, 2019 (the "Prime Contract")
**Architect:** Example Architecture, PLLC
**Subcontract Sum:** $1,820,000
**Date:** December 2, 2019

## Article 1. The Work

§ 1.1 Subcontractor shall perform the following work (the "Subcontract Work"): metal stud framing, gypsum board, blocking, access panels and soffits throughout.

§ 1.2 Subcontractor shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment and supervision for the Subcontract Work and shall coordinate with other trades as Contractor directs.

§ 1.3 The Subcontract Documents are this Agreement, the Prime Contract to the extent stated in Article 2, the Drawings and Specifications listed in Exhibit 1, and Modifications issued after the date of this Agreement.

## Article 2. Relationship to the Prime Contract

§ 2.1 To the extent of the Subcontract Work, Subcontractor assumes toward Contractor all obligations that Contractor assumes toward Owner and Architect under the Prime Contract, including its notice, claim and change procedures.

§ 2.2 Subcontractor shall not communicate directly with Owner or Architect about changes, claims or payment. All such communications go through Contractor.

## Article 3. Time

§ 3.1 Subcontractor shall perform the Subcontract Work in accordance with Contractor's project schedule as updated from time to time and shall supply crews sufficient to meet it.

§ 3.2 Contractor shall pay Subcontractor's documented extended field costs for delay caused by Contractor or its other subcontractors, and shall pass through Owner-caused delay costs as Owner pays them.

§ 3.3 Subcontractor shall give Contractor a two-week look-ahead schedule each Monday.

## Article 4. Changes

§ 4.1 Contractor may order changes in the Subcontract Work by written change directive. No other person may authorize a change.

§ 4.2 Subcontractor shall give Contractor written notice of any event, direction or condition that Subcontractor believes changes its cost or time within 3 days after Subcontractor first learns of it. The notice shall describe the event, the document or direction involved, and a preliminary estimate of cost and time. Subcontractor waives any adjustment for costs incurred before timely notice is given.

§ 4.3 Subcontractor shall submit a complete, itemized price for a change within 7 days after Contractor's written request. The price shall show labor hours and rates, materials, equipment, lower-tier quotes with their own breakdowns, and markup.

§ 4.4 On changed work, Subcontractor may add overhead and profit of up to 10% on its own direct cost and up to 5% on the cost of work by its lower-tier subcontractors. No markup is allowed on markup.

§ 4.5 Time-and-material work requires tags signed each day by Contractor's superintendent. Unsigned tags are not accepted.

## Article 5. Payment

§ 5.1 Subcontractor shall submit a payment application by the 20th of each month for Work completed through that date, with conditional lien waivers for itself and its lower-tier subcontractors.

§ 5.2 Contractor shall pay Subcontractor within 10 days after Contractor receives payment from Owner, and in any event within 45 days after Subcontractor's application; receipt of Owner payment is not a condition precedent to payment.

§ 5.3 Contractor shall retain 5% of each progress payment and release it when Owner releases retainage under the Prime Contract.

## Article 6. Insurance, Warranty and Disputes

§ 6.1 Subcontractor shall carry commercial general liability insurance of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Contractor and Owner as additional insureds.

§ 6.2 Subcontractor warrants the Subcontract Work for one year after Substantial Completion of the Project.

§ 6.3 Disputes that involve acts of Owner or Architect are resolved under the Prime Contract's dispute procedures. Other disputes go to mediation and then to the Superior Court of King County, Washington.

## Signatures

Contractor: Example Builders, Inc., by its Project Manager

Subcontractor: Evergreen Framing, by an authorized officer
