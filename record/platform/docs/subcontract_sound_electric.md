# Trade Subcontract: Electrical

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** A fictional subcontract written with AI assistance for INFO 492 C coursework. No real firm, person or project is represented.

**Contractor:** Example Builders, Inc. ("Contractor"), GC/CM for the Project
**Subcontractor:** Sound Electric ("Subcontractor")
**Project:** School Modernization and Addition, for Example School District No. 1 ("Owner")
**Prime Contract:** Agreement Between Owner and Construction Manager as Constructor (GC/CM) dated June 17, 2019 (the "Prime Contract")
**Architect:** Example Architecture, PLLC
**Subcontract Sum:** $1,780,000
**Date:** November 25, 2019

¶ 1. **Work.** Electrical service, power, lighting, low-voltage pathways and seismic bracing of electrical systems, as shown and specified, including all items reasonably inferable as needed for a complete installation.

¶ 2. **Price.** The Subcontract Sum stated above includes all taxes except Washington retail sales tax on the Subcontract Sum, which Contractor pays.

¶ 3. **Prime Contract.** Subcontractor is bound to Contractor by every term of the Prime Contract that applies to the Subcontract Work, including the notice and change provisions.

¶ 4. **Schedule.** Subcontractor shall follow Contractor's schedule and attend the weekly subcontractor coordination meeting.

¶ 5. **Billing.** Bill monthly on the 20th on Contractor's form, with lien releases.

¶ 6. **Payment.** Owner's payment to Contractor is not a condition precedent to payment to Subcontractor; Contractor shall pay within 30 days after a proper application. Retainage is 5%.

¶ 7. **Safety.** Subcontractor shall follow Contractor's site safety plan and Washington safety rules, and shall attend the weekly safety meeting.

¶ 8. **Changes.** Only Contractor's Project Manager may order a change, and only in writing.

¶ 9. **Notice.** Subcontractor must notify Contractor in writing within 3 days of any instruction, RFI response, submittal comment or site condition it believes is a change. Notice must say what happened and give a rough cost and time estimate.

¶ 10. **Pricing.** Change pricing is due 7 days after Contractor asks for it. Pricing must break out labor, material, equipment and lower-tier costs.

¶ 11. **Markup.** Markup on changed work: 10% on Subcontractor's own costs, 10% on lower-tier costs, and nothing on markup.

¶ 12. **Time and material.** Tags must be signed by Contractor's superintendent on the day the work is done.

¶ 13. **Delay.** Contractor shall pay Subcontractor's documented extended field costs for delay caused by Contractor or its other subcontractors, and shall pass through Owner-caused delay costs as Owner pays them.

¶ 14. **Insurance.** General liability of $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate; Contractor and Owner are additional insureds.

¶ 15. **Warranty.** One year after Substantial Completion.

¶ 16. **Cleanup and protection.** Subcontractor shall clean up its debris daily, protect its installed work and the work of others until Substantial Completion, and repair any damage it causes at its own cost.

¶ 17. **Submittals and closeout.** Subcontractor shall send product data, shop drawings and samples to Contractor in time to meet the submittal schedule, and at closeout shall deliver operation and maintenance data, warranties and marked-up record drawings for the Subcontract Work.

¶ 18. **Disputes.** Disputes that involve acts of Owner or Architect follow the Prime Contract's dispute procedures. Other disputes go to mediation, then King County Superior Court.

Accepted for Contractor (Example Builders, Inc.) and Subcontractor (Sound Electric).
