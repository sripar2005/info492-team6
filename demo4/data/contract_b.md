# Agreement Between Owner and Construction Manager as Constructor (GC/CM)

*Negotiated Guaranteed Maximum Price form, with Owner supplementary conditions.*

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** This is a fictional contract written for INFO 492 C coursework. It is not the contract of any real project, owner, or firm. Names, dates, and amounts are invented.

**Project:** Middle School Replacement (the "Project")
**Owner:** Example School District No. 2 ("Owner")
**Construction Manager:** Example Constructors, LLC ("CM")
**Architect:** Example Design Studio, Inc. ("Architect")
**Effective Date:** March 2, 2021
**Guaranteed Maximum Price:** $31,875,000

## Article 1. General Provisions

**1.1 Contract Documents.** The Contract Documents consist of this Agreement, the Supplementary Conditions, the Drawings, the Specifications, all Addenda issued before execution, and all Modifications issued after execution. The Contract Documents form the entire agreement between Owner and CM and supersede prior negotiations, proposals, and understandings.

**1.2 Interpretation.** Where the Contract Documents conflict, executed Modifications govern over this Agreement, this Agreement governs over the Supplementary Conditions, and the Supplementary Conditions govern over the Drawings and Specifications. Headings are for convenience only and do not limit the text that follows them.

**1.3 Definitions.** Capitalized terms have the following meanings. A "Change Order" is a written Modification signed by Owner, Architect, and CM adjusting the Guaranteed Maximum Price, the Contract Time, or both. A "Change Proposal" is CM's written, priced request for such an adjustment. A "Construction Change Directive" is a written order from Owner directing a change before its price or time is agreed. A "Potential Change" is any RFI response, Architect's Supplemental Instruction, submittal return, direction, physical condition, or other event that CM believes will increase its cost or time of performance. "General Conditions Costs" means the field supervision, site office, and site support costs described in Section 5.3.

**1.4 Computation of Time.** Unless otherwise stated, "days" means calendar days. When the last day of a period falls on a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday recognized by the State of Washington, the period runs until the end of the next day that is not a Saturday, Sunday, or legal holiday.

## Article 2. Construction Manager's Services

**2.1 Preconstruction Phase.** During the preconstruction phase CM shall review the design for constructability, prepare cost estimates at the schematic, design development, and construction documents milestones, and advise Owner on phasing, long-lead procurement, and site logistics. CM shall be paid for preconstruction services the lump sum of $186,500, which is not part of the Guaranteed Maximum Price.

**2.2 Construction Phase.** CM shall furnish all labor, materials, equipment, and services necessary to complete the Work in accordance with the Contract Documents, and shall supervise and direct the Work using its best skill and attention.

**2.3 Schedule.** Within 21 working days after the Notice to Proceed CM shall submit a critical path method schedule for the Work in a native scheduling format acceptable to Owner. CM shall update the schedule monthly, as of the last working day of the month, and shall identify in each update any activity whose total float has decreased by more than five working days since the prior update.

**2.4 Self-Performed Work.** CM may perform portions of the Work with its own forces only where its sealed bid for that portion was the lowest responsive bid. Self-performed work shall not exceed 25% of the combined cost of subcontracted and self-performed work over the life of the Project.

**2.5 No Unauthorized Changes.** No RFI response, meeting minute, email, or oral statement authorizes a change in the Guaranteed Maximum Price or the Contract Time. CM proceeds with changed Work before a Change Order or Construction Change Directive at its own risk.

## Article 3. Contract Time

**3.1 Commencement.** The date of commencement is the date stated in Owner's Notice to Proceed.

**3.2 Substantial Completion.** CM shall achieve Substantial Completion of the entire Work not later than 420 days after the date of commencement, subject to adjustment only by Change Order. CM shall achieve Final Completion within 45 days after the date of Substantial Completion.

**3.3 Liquidated Damages.** Owner and CM agree that Owner's actual damages for late completion would be difficult to determine. If CM fails to achieve Substantial Completion within the Contract Time, CM shall pay Owner, as liquidated damages and not as a penalty, $1,800 for each day that Substantial Completion is late. No liquidated damages accrue for failure to achieve Final Completion, but Owner may recover its actual costs of administering the Project after the Final Completion date.

**3.4 Extensions of Time.** The Contract Time may be extended only by Change Order and only for a delay that is beyond the control and without the fault of CM and its Subcontractors, that is shown by a time impact analysis to delay an activity on the critical path of the most recent accepted schedule update, and for which CM has given timely notice under Section 7.2. Float in the schedule is a shared resource and is not for the exclusive benefit of either party.

**3.5 Weather.** Precipitation, wind, or temperature that exceeds the ten-year average for the month at the nearest NOAA station, and that prevents work on the critical path, is a delay beyond CM's control. CM shall document each such day in its daily report.

## Article 4. Guaranteed Maximum Price

**4.1 Amount.** The Guaranteed Maximum Price is $31,875,000. It includes the Cost of the Work, CM's Fee, the General Conditions Costs, and the CM Contingency. CM shall bear any cost that would cause the Guaranteed Maximum Price to be exceeded except as adjusted by Change Order.

**4.2 CM Contingency.** The Guaranteed Maximum Price includes a CM Contingency of $425,000 for costs within CM's reasonable control, such as buyout shortfalls, coordination errors among trades, and minor scope gaps between subcontracts. CM shall notify Owner in writing before each use. The CM Contingency is not available for Owner-requested changes, unforeseen site conditions, or delays caused by Owner or Architect.

**4.3 Savings.** Savings below the Guaranteed Maximum Price at final accounting shall be shared, with 80% to Owner and the balance to CM.

## Article 5. Compensation

**5.1 Construction Phase Fee.** CM's Fee for the Work described in the Contract Documents at execution is the fixed amount of $1,214,600. The Fee is adjusted only for Change Orders and only as provided in Section 7.5.

**5.2 Cost of the Work.** The Cost of the Work means the costs necessarily incurred by CM in the proper performance of the Work, including payments to Subcontractors, materials and equipment incorporated in the Work, CM's field labor, and the premiums for bonds and insurance required by Article 11. Wages of CM's field labor shall be paid at the rates in the approved labor rate schedule plus a fixed labor burden equal to 30% of direct wages, which covers payroll taxes, workers' compensation, benefits, and union fringes.

**5.3 General Conditions Costs.** The General Conditions Costs are a fixed sum of $1,286,000, covering the project manager, superintendents, project engineers, safety staff, field office, temporary toilets, project vehicles, and small tools. If the Contract Time is extended by Change Order for a delay for which CM is entitled to compensation under Section 7.5.6, the General Conditions Costs shall be increased at the rate of $2,450 for each day of the extension, with no Fee added.

**5.4 Costs Not Reimbursed.** The following are included in the Fee or the General Conditions Costs and shall not be reimbursed separately, including in a Change Proposal: salaries of CM's officers and home office staff; home office expenses; estimating and purchasing; any position listed in Section 5.3; small tools with a replacement value under $1,500 per item; and the cost of preparing Change Proposals and claims.

## Article 6. Payment

**6.1 Applications for Payment.** CM shall submit a monthly Application for Payment on or before the 20th day of each month for Work performed through the end of the prior month, together with the updated schedule, the change order log, and conditional lien releases from each Subcontractor paid in the prior month.

**6.2 Progress Payments.** Owner shall pay each approved Application for Payment within 30 days after receipt of a complete application. Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at 1% per month.

**6.3 Retainage.** Owner shall withhold retainage of 5% from each progress payment as required by RCW 60.28. Retainage shall be released after completion of the Work and receipt of the releases required by statute.

**6.4 Final Payment.** Final payment, including retainage, is due when Final Completion is achieved, all closeout submittals have been received and accepted, and the statutory lien period has expired without claims.

## Article 7. Changes in the Work

**7.1 Change Instruments.** Owner may order changes in the Work by Change Order or Construction Change Directive without invalidating this Agreement. Architect may order minor changes not involving an adjustment in the Guaranteed Maximum Price or the Contract Time by Architect's Supplemental Instruction.

**7.2 Notice of Potential Change.** CM shall give written notice of a Potential Change within 10 days after it receives an RFI response, Architect's Supplemental Instruction, submittal return, or written or oral direction from Owner or Architect, or after it first observes a concealed or unknown physical condition, which in that case it shall not disturb before giving notice. For any other event that may increase its cost or time, CM shall give notice within 21 days after it knew or reasonably should have known of the event. Each notice shall describe the event, identify the documents involved, and give CM's preliminary estimate of cost and time. A notice that is not timely waives any adjustment for costs incurred before the notice and may waive any adjustment of the Contract Time.

**7.3 Change Proposals.** CM shall submit a complete Change Proposal within 30 days after giving notice of a Potential Change, or within 14 days after receiving a Proposal Request from Architect. Each Change Proposal shall reference the event that caused it and the date of notice, include itemized labor, material, and equipment costs and each Subcontractor's quote with its own breakdown, and state the number of days of any time extension requested, supported by a time impact analysis.

**7.4 Review.** Architect shall review each complete Change Proposal and send Owner's response within 15 days after receipt. A Change Proposal that is missing required backup shall be returned within 5 working days, identifying what is missing. Silence by Owner is not acceptance.

**7.5 Pricing of Changes.** Each Change Order and Construction Change Directive shall be priced under this Section 7.5.

**7.5.1 Direct Costs.** Direct costs are CM's field labor with the burden stated in Section 5.2, materials at net cost after discounts and rebates, equipment under Section 7.5.4, and Subcontractor costs under Section 7.5.2.

**7.5.2 Subcontractor Markup.** A Subcontractor may add a combined allowance for overhead and profit of not more than 8% of the cost of the changed work it performs with its own forces, and not more than 4% of the cost of changed work performed by its lower-tier subcontractors. In no event shall the cumulative markup of all tiers on any portion of the changed work exceed 12%.

**7.5.3 CM's Fee on Changes.** On each Change Order CM may add a Fee of 4.5%, computed only on CM direct costs and subcontractor quotes. No Fee shall be added to the General Conditions Costs, bond and insurance premiums, sales and business taxes, or permit fees. On a deductive Change Order the Fee shall be reduced at the same rate.

**7.5.4 Equipment.** Equipment owned by CM or by an entity affiliated with CM shall be charged at 70% of the monthly, weekly, or daily rental rate, whichever is lowest for the duration of use, published in the current edition of the Equipment Watch Rental Rate Blue Book, without separate charges for fuel or maintenance. Rented equipment shall be charged at the invoice cost. Standby time shall be charged at 50% of the applicable rate and only when the standby is caused by the change.

**7.5.5 Time-and-Material Work.** Work performed on a time-and-material basis shall be recorded on daily tickets signed by CM's superintendent and Owner's field representative within 2 working days after the work is performed. Unsigned tickets will not be accepted as support for payment.

**7.5.6 Delay Compensation.** CM is entitled to an increase in the General Conditions Costs for an extension of the Contract Time only when the delay was caused by Owner, or by a separate contractor or consultant retained by Owner other than Architect, and the delay affected the critical path. A delay caused by Architect's acts or omissions entitles CM to an extension of the Contract Time under Section 3.4 but not to any increase in the Guaranteed Maximum Price. When a delay to the critical path has concurrent causes and one of them is the responsibility of CM or its Subcontractors, CM shall receive an extension of time but no additional compensation for the period of concurrency.

**7.6 Construction Change Directives.** CM shall promptly proceed with Work directed by a Construction Change Directive. If the parties do not agree on the adjustment within 45 days after the Change Proposal is submitted, Architect shall determine the adjustment on the basis of the reasonable cost of the changed Work priced under Section 7.5, and CM may dispute the determination under Article 10.

**7.7 Authority to Approve.** The Owner's Representative may execute any Change Order whose value does not exceed $150,000. Any larger Change Order, or a group of related Change Orders whose combined value exceeds that limit, requires approval by resolution of Owner's Board of Directors, which meets on the second and fourth Tuesdays of each month.

**7.8 Change Order Log.** CM shall maintain a log of all notices, Change Proposals, Construction Change Directives, and Change Orders, showing for each its number, dates, amount, time requested, and status. CM shall submit the log with each Application for Payment.

## Article 8. Requests for Information

**8.1 Form.** CM shall submit each RFI on Owner's standard form, citing the affected drawings and specification sections, stating the question and CM's proposed answer, and giving the date by which a response is needed to avoid delay.

**8.2 Response.** Architect shall respond to each RFI within 7 days after receipt. An RFI that CM identifies as affecting an activity on the critical path shall be answered within 3 working days. An RFI response does not change the Guaranteed Maximum Price or the Contract Time; if CM believes that it does, CM shall proceed under Section 7.2.

**8.3 Unnecessary RFIs.** If Architect determines that an RFI could have been answered from the Contract Documents, Architect may return it without a full response. After the fifteenth such RFI, Architect's review time may be charged to CM at $275 per RFI.

## Article 9. Submittals

**9.1 Submittal Schedule.** CM shall submit a submittal schedule within 30 days after the Notice to Proceed that identifies every item with a procurement lead time over 16 weeks.

**9.2 Review.** Architect shall review each submittal within 14 days after receipt and each resubmittal within 7 days. A submittal returned "Revise and Resubmit" twice for the same reason shall be reviewed at CM's cost on its third submission.

## Article 10. Claims and Disputes

**10.1 Claims.** A claim for an adjustment of the Guaranteed Maximum Price or the Contract Time that is not resolved through a Change Proposal shall be submitted in writing within 30 days after Owner's written rejection of the related Change Proposal. A claim not submitted within that period is waived.

**10.2 Decision and Mediation.** Owner shall issue a written decision on a claim within 45 days after receipt. A party that disputes the decision may demand mediation within 60 days, and the parties shall share the mediator's fees equally.

**10.3 Litigation.** Disputes not resolved by mediation shall be resolved in the Superior Court of Snohomish County, Washington. CM shall continue the Work and Owner shall continue to pay undisputed amounts while a dispute is pending.

## Article 11. Insurance and Bonds

**11.1 Bonds.** CM shall furnish a performance bond and a payment bond, each in an amount equal to 100% of the Guaranteed Maximum Price, issued by a surety admitted in Washington.

**11.2 Insurance.** CM shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $4,000,000 general aggregate, business automobile liability insurance with a combined single limit of $1,000,000, and umbrella liability insurance of $5,000,000. Owner, Architect, and their officers and employees shall be named as additional insureds on a primary and non-contributory basis.

**11.3 Builder's Risk.** CM shall provide builder's risk insurance on an all-risk basis for the full replacement value of the Work, with a deductible not greater than $25,000 per occurrence.

## Article 12. Warranty

**12.1 Correction Period.** CM warrants that the Work is free from defects in materials and workmanship. CM shall correct defective Work that appears within 2 years after the date of Substantial Completion, promptly after receiving written notice from Owner.

**12.2 Manufacturer Warranties.** CM shall obtain and assign to Owner all manufacturer warranties, including a 20-year no-dollar-limit warranty on the roofing system and a 10-year warranty on the curtain wall and storefront glazing units.

## Article 13. Records and Audit

**13.1 Records.** CM shall keep complete cost records, including Subcontractor records relating to changed Work, for 6 years after final payment.

**13.2 Audit.** Owner may audit CM's records during that period on 5 working days' notice. Overcharges found by audit shall be refunded within 30 days after the audit report is issued, and if they exceed 2% of the audited amount CM shall also pay the reasonable cost of the audit.

## Article 14. Termination and Suspension

**14.1 Termination for Cause.** Owner may terminate this Agreement for cause if CM fails to cure a material breach within 7 days after receiving written notice of the breach.

**14.2 Termination for Convenience.** Owner may terminate this Agreement for convenience on 14 days' written notice. CM shall then be paid for Work properly performed, reasonable demobilization costs, and the Fee earned on that Work, but not for anticipated profit on Work not performed.

**14.3 Suspension.** Owner may suspend the Work for up to 90 consecutive days by written notice. A suspension for which CM is not responsible entitles CM to an adjustment under Article 7.

## Article 15. Miscellaneous

**15.1 Governing Law.** This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington.

**15.2 Notices.** Notices shall be in writing and delivered to the representatives designated by each party. Notice through the project management platform designated by Owner is written notice, received on the next working day after it is posted.

**15.3 Assignment.** Neither party shall assign this Agreement without the written consent of the other, except that Owner may assign it to a successor school district.

**15.4 No Waiver.** Payment by Owner or failure to object does not waive any right under this Agreement.
