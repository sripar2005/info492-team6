# GC/CM Contract for Construction Services

*Heating, Ventilation, and Seismic Improvements. Maximum Allowable Construction Cost (MACC) form, with District Supplementary Conditions in Article 16.*

> **SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.** This is a fictional contract written for INFO 492 C coursework as held-out test data. It is not the contract of any real project, school district, or firm. Names, dates, and amounts are invented.

**Project:** Example Middle School HVAC Replacement and Seismic Retrofit (the "Project")
**Owner:** Example School District No. 3 (the "District")
**GC/CM:** Example Contractors Northwest, Inc. (the "Contractor")
**Architect:** Example Engineering + Architecture, P.S. (the "Architect")
**Contract Date:** January 11, 2022
**Total Contract Cost:** $14,944,000

## ARTICLE 1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

**§ 1.1 Contract Documents.** The Contract Documents are this Contract, including the Supplementary Conditions in Article 16 and Exhibits A and B, the Drawings, the Specifications, Addenda issued before the Contract Date, and Modifications issued after it.

**§ 1.2 Order of Precedence.** In case of conflict, the Supplementary Conditions in Article 16 govern over Articles 1 through 15, the Articles govern over the Exhibits, and the Exhibits govern over the Drawings and Specifications. Figures govern over scaled dimensions. A later-dated Modification governs over an earlier one.

**§ 1.3 Definitions.** "Day" means a calendar day unless it is called a Working Day. "Working Day" means Monday through Friday, excluding District holidays and days on which the District closes the school for weather. "Change Order Proposal" or "COP" means the Contractor's priced request to adjust the Total Contract Cost or the Contract Time. "Field Order" means a written order by the District's Project Manager directing minor work at a stated price. "Potential Change" means any RFI response, supplemental instruction, submittal return, direction, physical condition, or other event that the Contractor believes will increase its cost or time. "MACC" means the Maximum Allowable Construction Cost for the Work stated in Section 4.1. "Specified General Conditions" means the fixed-price field management and site costs listed in Exhibit B.

**§ 1.4 Headings.** Headings and the numbering of paragraphs are for reference only.

## ARTICLE 2. PRECONSTRUCTION SERVICES

**§ 2.1 Scope.** Before construction the Contractor reviewed the design for constructability and phasing on an occupied campus, prepared estimates at each design milestone, and bid the subcontract packages under RCW 39.10. The District shall pay a lump sum of $142,000 for these services, which is not part of the Total Contract Cost.

**§ 2.2 Long-Lead Equipment.** The Contractor shall release orders for the rooftop units, the dedicated outdoor air units, and the boiler plant within ten (10) Working Days after approval of the related submittals.

## ARTICLE 3. CONTRACT TIME

**§ 3.1 Commencement.** The Contract Time begins on the date stated in the District's Notice to Proceed for Construction.

**§ 3.2 Completion.** The Contractor shall achieve Substantial Completion of the entire Work within four hundred fifty-five (455) calendar days after the date of the Notice to Proceed for Construction. Phase 1 (Building B classrooms) shall be ready for student occupancy within one hundred ninety (190) calendar days after that date. Final Completion shall follow within forty-five (45) days after Substantial Completion.

**§ 3.3 Liquidated Damages.** The parties agree that the District's damages from late completion are difficult to measure. For each calendar day after the Contract Time that Substantial Completion has not been achieved, the Contractor shall pay the District Two Thousand Two Hundred Dollars ($2,200) as liquidated damages and not as a penalty. Separately, if Phase 1 is not ready for student occupancy by its milestone date, the Contractor shall pay Seven Hundred Fifty Dollars ($750) per calendar day until it is.

**§ 3.4 Occupied Campus.** The school remains in session during most of the Work. Noisy work, roof crane picks, and shutdowns of heating, power, or the fire alarm in occupied buildings shall be done after 3:30 p.m., on weekends, or during school breaks, as shown on the phasing plan. The Contractor shall give the District seventy-two (72) hours' notice of any planned shutdown.

**§ 3.5 Construction Schedule.** Within fourteen (14) days after the Notice to Proceed for Construction the Contractor shall submit a critical path method schedule in native Primavera P6 format. The Contractor shall update it monthly, identify the critical path in each update, and show the float of every activity. The District's acceptance of a schedule does not approve any sequence or any request for time.

## ARTICLE 4. TOTAL CONTRACT COST

**§ 4.1 MACC and Total Contract Cost.** The MACC is $12,100,000. The Total Contract Cost of $14,944,000 is the sum of the MACC, the Contractor's Fee, the Specified General Conditions, the Negotiated Support Services, and the Contractor Contingency, as shown in Exhibit A.

**§ 4.2 Contractor Contingency.** The Total Contract Cost includes a Contractor Contingency of Three Hundred Ten Thousand Dollars ($310,000) for costs within the Contractor's reasonable control, such as buyout shortfalls and coordination gaps between subcontracts. It is not available for District-directed changes or for concealed conditions.

**§ 4.3 Savings.** Unspent MACC and Contractor Contingency at final accounting shall be shared sixty percent (60%) to the District and forty percent (40%) to the Contractor.

## ARTICLE 5. COMPENSATION

**§ 5.1 Contractor's Fee.** The Contractor's Fee for the Work at the Contract Date is five and one-quarter percent (5.25%) of the MACC. The Fee is adjusted for changes only as stated in Section 9.4.3.

**§ 5.2 Cost of the Work.** The Cost of the Work includes payments to Subcontractors, materials and equipment incorporated in the Work, sales tax, and the wages of the Contractor's craft workers at the rates in the approved labor rate sheet, plus a labor burden of thirty-five percent (35%) of base wages for payroll taxes, industrial insurance, benefits, and union fringes. Overtime premium is reimbursed only when the District approves the overtime in writing in advance.

**§ 5.3 Specified General Conditions.** The Specified General Conditions are a lump sum of $1,412,750 and include the project manager, superintendent, project engineer, safety coordinator, field office, temporary toilets, and project vehicles. If the Contract Time is extended by Change Order for a delay for which the Contractor is entitled to compensation under Section 9.4.7, the Specified General Conditions shall be increased by $2,975.00 per calendar day of the extension. No Fee is added to that increase.

**§ 5.4 Negotiated Support Services.** Negotiated Support Services, including temporary heat, dust partitions in occupied corridors, and crane mobilizations, are a lump sum of $486,000 and are adjusted only by Change Order.

**§ 5.5 Costs Not Reimbursed.** The following are included in the Fee or the Specified General Conditions and shall not be billed separately or included in a COP: home office salaries and expenses; estimating; the positions listed in Section 5.3; small tools with a replacement value under $500 per item; and the cost of preparing COPs and claims.

## ARTICLE 6. PAYMENT

**§ 6.1 Applications.** The Contractor shall submit a monthly application for payment by the 10th day of the month for Work performed through the end of the prior month, with the updated schedule and the COP log.

**§ 6.2 Payment.** The District shall pay each approved application within thirty (30) days after receipt. Undisputed amounts not paid when due bear interest at one percent (1%) per month.

**§ 6.3 Retainage.** The District shall retain five percent (5%) of each progress payment under RCW 60.28.

## ARTICLE 7. RFIs AND SUBMITTALS

**§ 7.1 RFIs.** The Architect shall respond to each RFI within eight (8) Working Days after receipt, or within two (2) Working Days for an RFI that the Contractor marks as affecting a shutdown window or the critical path. RFIs that could have been answered from the Contract Documents may be charged to the Contractor at $300 each after the twelfth such RFI.

**§ 7.2 Submittals.** The Architect shall review each submittal within ten (10) Working Days and each resubmittal within seven (7) Working Days. Delay resulting from the Architect's review of a resubmittal made necessary by the Contractor's incomplete or nonconforming submittal shall not be a basis for an adjustment of the Contract Time or the Total Contract Cost.

**§ 7.3 Effect.** No RFI response, submittal review, or meeting minute changes the Total Contract Cost or the Contract Time. If the Contractor believes one does, it shall proceed under Section 9.2.

## ARTICLE 8. DELAYS AND EXTENSIONS OF TIME

**§ 8.1 Weather.** The Contract Time includes fifteen (15) Working Days of anticipated adverse weather. Adverse weather beyond that allowance, documented in the Contractor's daily reports and compared with the ten-year average at the nearest NOAA station, is a cause beyond the Contractor's control.

**§ 8.2 Contractor's Responsibility.** Delay caused by the Contractor, its Subcontractors, or suppliers at any tier, including late submittals, lack of manpower, and equipment breakdowns, does not entitle the Contractor to an extension of time or to compensation.

**§ 8.3 Extensions of Time.**

**§ 8.3.1 Grounds.** If the Contractor is delayed at any time in the progress of the Work by an act or neglect of the District or the Architect, by changes ordered in the Work, by concealed conditions, by labor disputes, fire, unusual delay in deliveries, unavoidable casualties, or other causes beyond the Contractor's control, the Contract Time shall be extended by Change Order for such reasonable time as the District's Project Manager may determine.

**§ 8.3.2 Requests.** A request for an extension shall be made in the notice required by Section 9.2 and stated in days in the related COP, with a written narrative and such schedule information as the District's Project Manager may reasonably require.

**§ 8.3.3 Float.** Float belongs to the Project. The Contractor shall not sequester float through preferential logic or inflated durations.

## ARTICLE 9. CHANGES IN THE WORK

**§ 9.1 Change Instruments.** The District may order changes by Change Order or by Field Order. The Architect may order minor changes that do not change the Total Contract Cost or the Contract Time by supplemental instruction.

**§ 9.2 Notice of Potential Change.** The Contractor shall give the District written notice of a Potential Change within five (5) days after receipt of an RFI response, Architect's supplemental instruction, submittal return, or direction from the District or the Architect, or after first observing a concealed or unknown physical condition at the site, which the Contractor shall not disturb until the District has had an opportunity to observe it. For any other event or circumstance on which the Contractor intends to base a request for additional cost or time, notice shall be given within twenty (20) days after the Contractor knew or should have known of it. Late notice waives any adjustment for costs incurred before notice and may waive any adjustment of the Contract Time.

**§ 9.3 Change Order Proposals.** The Contractor shall submit a complete COP within fifteen (15) days after giving notice, with itemized labor, material, and equipment costs, each Subcontractor's quote with its own breakdown, and the number of days of any time requested. The District shall respond within twenty-one (21) days after receipt of a complete COP.

**§ 9.4 Pricing of Changes.**

**§ 9.4.1 Direct Costs.** The Contractor's direct costs are its craft labor with the burden stated in Section 5.2, materials at net cost, and equipment under Section 9.4.5.

**§ 9.4.2 Subcontractor Markup.** For changed Work performed by a Subcontractor with its own forces, the Subcontractor's overhead and profit shall not exceed twelve percent (12%) of its direct cost; for changed Work performed by its lower-tier subcontractors, the Subcontractor may add six percent (6%) to the lower-tier cost. The total of all overhead and profit markups by all tiers on any item of changed Work, excluding the Contractor's Fee, shall not exceed seventeen percent (17%).

**§ 9.4.3 Contractor's Fee on Changes.** The Contractor's Fee on a Change Order shall be four percent (4%) of the sum of the Contractor's direct costs, Subcontractor quotes as marked up under Section 9.4.2, and the premiums for bonds and insurance attributable to the change. No Fee is applied to the Specified General Conditions, the Negotiated Support Services, sales tax, or permit fees. On a deductive change the credit includes Fee at the same rate.

**§ 9.4.4 Labor Rates.** Labor shall be priced at the rates in the approved labor rate sheet. Foremen are chargeable only for time spent directing the changed Work.

**§ 9.4.5 Equipment.** Equipment owned by the Contractor or an affiliate is charged at eighty percent (80%) of the rates in the current Equipment Watch Rental Rate Blue Book, using the lowest of the monthly, weekly, or daily rate that fits the period of use. Standby is charged at fifty percent (50%) of that rate, and only when the standby is caused by the change. Rented equipment is charged at invoice cost.

**§ 9.4.6 Time-and-Material Work.** Tickets for time-and-material work shall be signed by the Contractor's superintendent and the District's site representative by the end of the following Working Day.

**§ 9.4.7 Delay Compensation.** The Contractor shall be compensated for extended Specified General Conditions under Section 5.3 only for a delay caused by the District, by the Architect, or by a concealed physical condition at the site that differs materially from the Contract Documents. Delay caused by weather, labor disputes, or other causes beyond the control of both parties entitles the Contractor to time only. Where a delay results from concurrent causes, one attributable to the District and one to the Contractor, the extension shall be granted for the full period of delay, but the compensation for extended Specified General Conditions shall be apportioned between the District and the Contractor in proportion to their respective contributions to the delay.

**§ 9.5 Field Orders.** The District's site representative may issue Field Orders for work not exceeding Seven Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($7,500) each. Field Orders are incorporated into the next Change Order.

**§ 9.6 Approval Authority.** Change Orders whose individual value does not exceed One Hundred Thousand Dollars ($100,000) may be executed by the District's Capital Projects Director. Any larger Change Order, and any Change Order that brings the cumulative total of Change Orders to more than five percent (5%) of the MACC, requires approval by the District's Board of Directors.

**§ 9.7 Change Log.** The Contractor shall keep a log of notices, COPs, Field Orders, and Change Orders and submit it with each application for payment.

## ARTICLE 10. CLAIMS AND DISPUTES

**§ 10.1 Claims.** A claim not resolved through a COP shall be submitted in writing within thirty (30) days after the District's written rejection of the COP, or it is waived.

**§ 10.2 Resolution.** The District shall decide a claim within forty-five (45) days. Unresolved disputes go to mediation and then to the Superior Court of Pierce County, Washington. The Contractor shall continue the Work during any dispute.

## ARTICLE 11. INSURANCE AND BONDS

**§ 11.1 Bonds.** The Contractor shall furnish performance and payment bonds, each for one hundred percent (100%) of the Total Contract Cost.

**§ 11.2 Insurance.** The Contractor shall maintain commercial general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and $3,000,000 aggregate, automobile liability of $1,000,000, and umbrella coverage of $5,000,000, naming the District and the Architect as additional insureds. Builder's risk coverage shall have a deductible of not more than $10,000.

## ARTICLE 12. WARRANTY

**§ 12.1 Correction Period.** The Contractor shall correct defective Work that appears within one (1) year after Substantial Completion. The Contractor shall obtain a five-year parts warranty on the compressors of the rooftop units.

## ARTICLE 13. RECORDS AND AUDIT

**§ 13.1 Records and Audit.** The Contractor shall keep cost records for six (6) years after final payment. If an audit finds overcharges of more than three percent (3%) of the audited amount, the Contractor shall pay the cost of the audit.

## ARTICLE 14. TERMINATION

**§ 14.1 For Cause.** The District may terminate for cause if the Contractor fails to begin curing a default within ten (10) days after written notice.

**§ 14.2 For Convenience.** The District may terminate for convenience on seven (7) days' written notice, paying for Work performed and reasonable demobilization costs, but not anticipated profit.

## ARTICLE 15. MISCELLANEOUS

**§ 15.1 Public Works Requirements.** The Contractor shall pay prevailing wages and shall meet the apprenticeship utilization requirement of fifteen percent (15%) of labor hours under RCW 39.04.320.

**§ 15.2 Notices.** Notices shall be in writing. A notice posted to the District's project management platform is received on the next Working Day.

**§ 15.3 Governing Law.** This Contract is governed by the laws of the State of Washington.

## ARTICLE 16. SUPPLEMENTARY CONDITIONS

**§ 16.1 Effect.** The following Supplementary Conditions modify the Articles above. Where they conflict with the Articles, these Supplementary Conditions govern.

**§ 16.2 Shutdown Windows.** Section 3.4 is supplemented: no shutdown of heating in an occupied building shall occur when the outdoor temperature is forecast to fall below 40 degrees Fahrenheit.

**§ 16.3 Fee on Changes.** Section 9.4.3 is amended by deleting "four percent (4%)" and substituting "three and three-quarters percent (3.75%)". The Fee rate in Section 5.1 is not changed.

**§ 16.4 Retainage Bond.** The Contractor may substitute a retainage bond for retained funds with the District's written consent.
